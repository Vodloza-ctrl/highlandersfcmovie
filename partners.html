Why This Partnership

Reach that outlasts a single moment.

Direct association with one of Zimbabwe's most recognised sporting institutions, at a significant moment in the club's history.

Reach across premieres, streaming, community screenings and, subject to a broadcast licence, national television.

Access to Highlanders' national and diaspora supporter base — South Africa, the UK, Botswana, Australia, and beyond.

Full category exclusivity is reserved for Principal Partners, and on-screen placements are capped at 56 slots so the film keeps its authenticity.

The Value of Partnering

What your brand gets inside the film.

Four things every business owner should know before choosing a placement. Tap any graphic to see it full size.

01 · Where your brand is seen

One film, many windows.

Your brand travels with the film: the 4 December 2026 premieres in Bulawayo and Harare, the paid streaming release, community screenings in May 2027, and companion content — reels, trailers, a behind-the-scenes documentary, a movie podcast and legend interviews.

Television and a free YouTube release follow the paid windows, with YouTube last, so your placement keeps being seen. Television depends on a broadcast licence and isn't guaranteed.

02 · Placement, not interruption

A story audiences choose to watch.

Adverts get skipped; stories get watched. Your product, signage, dialogue mention or credit becomes part of the story itself.

Pay once, and your placement appears every time the film is watched.

03 · Find your fit

From a single scene to category exclusivity.

On-screen placements are limited to 56 slots across a Premium and an SME tier, all non-exclusive. Official Partners get guaranteed advertising inventory across streaming and screenings.

Principal Partners take a category exclusively — one brand per category, whether that's fashion, electronics, communication, banking or FMCG.

04 · Join before filming

Join before the cameras roll.

Placements are confirmed ahead of principal photography, which begins 20 October 2026. Get in touch early and we'll shape the right placement with you.

Partnership Tiers

Four ways to partner with the film.

Reveal a tier's investment range below, or download the full rate cards. All amounts are in USD.

A small number of major category leaders

Principal Partner

On-screen brand integration within the film — signage, wardrobe or product usage — locked ahead of principal photography. Prominent placement across trailer, premiere and press materials, a producer-credit conversation, first right of refusal on renewal, and premiere executive seating.

Category-exclusive · one brand per category
$15,000 – $25,000
The standard corporate sponsorship tier

Official Partner

A Premium logo appearance and a digital billboard in the film, plus guaranteed advertising across the streaming release and screenings, including pre/mid-roll on the streaming platform. Premiere-night mention and end-credits sponsor card. TV placement is not guaranteed. If a broadcast deal is secured, Official and Principal Partners are prioritised at no extra cost.

Not category-exclusive
$5,000 – $12,000
On-screen presence at a lower cost of entry

Product Placement Partner

Individual on-screen slots — product use, signage, dialogue mentions and end-credit listings — sold individually or in bundles. The full slot list and bundles are in the rate card below.

Non-exclusive · 56 slots in total
From $50 per slot
Partners tied to a specific moment

Event Partner

Presence and recognition tied to a specific moment — premiere night, a launch event or a screening — rather than the whole campaign.

Confirmed on request

Every partner, at every tier, receives brand association across the tier's agreed touchpoints, mentions across companion content, recognition in campaign communications, premiere executive seating (tier-dependent), and a single point of contact on our team throughout.
